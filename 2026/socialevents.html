Social events

On Wednesday, 14th, and Thursday, 15th, SISAP will host two major social events.

Wednesday, October 14th

The final session ends at 17:40. At around 18:00, we will walk 1.7 km from the faculty building to Moravské náměstí (Moravian Square).

The meeting point will be at (under) the statue of Jošt of Moravia.

  • Alternatively, you can take tram line 6 from the stop "Hrnčířská" to "Česká" (direction "Starý Lískovec"),
  • or tram line 1 from the stop "Hrnčířská" to "Moravské náměstí" (direction "Bystrc, Ečerova").
  • (Note: The "Česká" stop is closer to the statue than the "Moravské náměstí" stop.)

A guided tour of the historic city centre

... starts from the statue of Jošt at 18:30. Duration: approximately 80 minutes.

A small reception with a beer tasting (in Czechia à 0.3 l or 0.5 l)

... starts at approximately 20:00 in the local city brewery Pegas, located at address Jakubská 120/4. Certain meal and beer consumption is prepaid for conference participants here.
