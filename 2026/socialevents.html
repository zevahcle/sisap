Social events

On Wednesday, 14th, and Thursday, 15th, SISAP will host two major social events.

Important: Daytime public transport services (lines 1 to 84) stop running between roughly 22:30 and 23:20. After that, night bus lines N89 to N99 operate every night ‒ see the Public Transport in Brno on the Conference Venue page.

The tickets and the rules are the same for daytime and night-time public transport services (i.e., no extra fare for night buses).


Wednesday, October 14th

The final session ends at 17:40. At around 18:00, we will walk 1.7 km from the faculty building to Moravské náměstí (Moravian Square).

A guided tour of the historic city centre

The meeting point will be at (under) the statue of Jošt of Moravia.

  • Alternatively, you can take tram line 6 from the "Hrnčířská" stop to "Česká" (direction "Starý Lískovec"),
  • or tram line 1 from the "Hrnčířská" stop to "Moravské náměstí" (direction "Bystrc, Ečerova").
  • (Note: The "Česká" stop is closer to the statue than the "Moravské náměstí" stop.)

The tour starts from the statue of Jošt at 18:30. Duration: approximately 80 minutes.

A small reception with a beer tasting (up to 4 x à 0.2 l)

... starts after 20:00 in one of the local city breweries, Pegas, located at address Jakubská 120/4. Certain meal and beer tasting is prepaid for conference participants here.

  • The brewery is 650 m walk from the Hotel Continental.
  • Hotel Cosmopolitan (Boby Center) is 2.2 km far, and you can use:
    • the bus line 67 from the "Moravské náměstí" stop to "Sportovní" (direction "Jundrov"),
    • or the tram line 6 from the "Česká" stop to "Hrnčířská" (direction "Královo pole, nádraží"),
    • or the tram line 1 from the "Moravské náměstí" stop to "Hrnčířská" (direction "Řečkovice"),
  • The night buses N90 and N91 run between "Moravské náměstí" and "Hrnčířská" with departures 2 minutes after the departure from the Main Train Station. The directions are "Ořešín" (N90), and "Ivanovice, Globus" or "Lelekovice, u kříže" (N91).

Thursday, October 15th

The final session ends at 17:30. At around 17:55, we will walk 2.7 km from the faculty building to Mendlovo náměstí (Mendel Square).

A tour of the Mendel Museum

The meeting point will be in front of the entrance at 18:40.

  • To go to Mendel Square, you can take tram line 6 from the "Hrnčířská" stop to "Mendlovo náměstí" (direction "Starý Lískovec"),
  • The same tram line 6 (with the same stops) is to be used from Cosmopolitan hotel (Boby center)
  • If travelling from Continental hotel, you can use tram line 6 (direction "Starý Lískovec"), or tram line 5 (direction "Ústřední hřbitov"), both going from "Česká" stop to "Mendlovo náměstí.

Conference dinner with wine tasting (up to ???)

... starts after 20:00 the restaurant, U královny Elišky, located next to the Mendel museum.

  • The last direct tram (line 6) goes from Mendel square ("Mendlovo náměstí" stop) to "Česká" (hotel Continental) and "Hrnčířská" (hotel Cosmopolitan, Boby center) at 23:13.
  • From approx 22:40, you can use night buses.
  • Journey to hotel Continental: e.g., N98 goes from "Mendlovo náměstí" stop to "Hlavní nádraží" (Main train station) (direction "Mariánské údolí").
    You need to change to N93 at the Main tration station. Be aware of a direction: Komín sídliště. Two buses of the same number always meet at the Main Train Station.
  • Journey to hotel Cosmopolitan (Boby center):
    • If you want to avoid a change, go to "Poříčí" stop and take N91 (direction "Ivanovice, Globus" or "Lelekovice, u kříže") to "Hrnčířská" stop.
    • If you want to go from the Mendel square, take, e.g., N98 from "Mendlovo náměstí" stop to "Hlavní nádraží" (Main train station) (direction "Mariánské údolí"),
      and change, e.g., to
      N91 at the Main tration station. Be aware of a direction: "Ivanovice, Globus" OR "Lelekovice, u kříže". Two buses of the same number always meet at the Main Train Station.
