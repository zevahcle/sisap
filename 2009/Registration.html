Registration type Early registration

Author registration 7000 CZK not available (every paper must be registered before July 1st) Each accepted paper/poster/demo must be registered by this type of registration, in order to appear in the conference proceedings.
The author registration includes one regular participant registration.
Regular participant 7000 CZK 8000 CZK Includes admission to conference halls, conference bag, proceedings, coffee breaks, lunches, conference dinner, social event, public transport tickets, map.
Student participant 5000 CZK 6000 CZK Includes the same as regular registration, but is restricted only to full-time students up to PhD (included). A scanned valid ISIC card or confirmation of full-time student status signed by head of the department must be emailed to the organizers.
Accompanying person 3000 CZK 4000 CZK Non-conference participant (e.g., friend, partner). Includes conference dinner, social event, public transport tickets, map. Lunches are optional for extra charge.
  • All prices are in Czech Crowns (CZK), including VAT.
  • Payment at the registration desk is not possible.
Feel free to contact the organizers if necessary (sisap2009(at)gmail.com).
