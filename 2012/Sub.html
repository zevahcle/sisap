Instructions for camera-ready submissions

Your final paper can be up to 16 pages long (demo 2 pages), formatted according to the LNCS style rules, which must be followed strictly (Springer LNCS latex 2e macros). You will have to upload the sources, a pdf, and a copyright transfer form. The submissions will be made through the Easy Chair conference system.

For a limited number of papers an extension of up to two extra pages will be allowed (i.e., max total 18 pages). One extra page costs 50 CAD, two pages 100 CAD. When interested, contact the PC chairs as soon as possible by email (if granted, check also the right option in the registration form).

At least one author of each accepted paper must be registered to the conference by the deadline of June 1; the registration system will be opened some days before. Submissions without registered authors will have to be left out of the proceedings. In addition, at least one author of each paper is expected to attend the conference and present the paper.

Presentation at the conference

Full papers:
Accepted full papers are expected to be orally presented at the conference session. Each paper has allocated 25 minutes for presentation and 5 minutes for discussion.
Posters and demos:
- Accepted poster papers are expected to be presented at the conference poster session. A suitable form of poster is a large (A0-A1) piece of paper with concise schema sumarizing the paper contribution. We strongly discourage to use posters in form of printed papers.
- An accepted demo paper is expected to be presented at the conference demo session. The demo presentation will be performed on an on-site notebook (or the presenter’s notebook) with a high-speed connection to internet. Optionally, there will be a data projector and a projection wall available.
