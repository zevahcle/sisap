Registering for SISAP

This workshop is a satellite workshop of ICDE 2008
For SISAP, you need at least to register in the category "Affiliated Workshops Only", although you might want instead to take the combo with ICDE, "Conference and Workshops". The student categories have only the combo option. Although it is slightly more expensive than a regular registration for "Affiliated Workshops Only", students might well be interested in attending ICDE for basically the same price.
